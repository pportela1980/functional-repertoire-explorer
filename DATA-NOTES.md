## Source vs interpretation

Four provenance labels are used throughout the UI:

- **Source record** — API snapshot fields and report quotations. Spanish text is shown unchanged; no machine translation.
- **Reported episode** — episode framing (demand, reported response, reported continuity). English titles/summaries are analytical framing of report content.
- **Functional interpretation** — readings of an episode through a function (`functionalReadings`).
- **Analyst synthesis** — configuration groupings and the system-dynamics map.

Not in this version: predictions, scenarios, simulations, recommendations, scores, budgets. The `budget_period_pair` source field contains only an identifier; no amount is shown. Matrix numbers are counts of curated episodes. Proposals in the reports are labelled as proposals in the episode texts.

People: no fictional people. Perception records that are individual responses carry personal names in the source; the UI shows them as "Individual perception response #id" (data minimisation). Free-text fields of listening records sometimes contain participant lists; `scripts/build-data.mjs` replaces runs of name-like lines (3+ lines, or any name lines after a "Participantes" header, or a field consisting only of names) with "[N personal names removed]" (124 lines in 27 blocks in this snapshot; run with `SHOW_REDACTED=1` to review). The heuristic is not guaranteed to catch every name inside running prose. The raw pack under `reference/` is unredacted and should not be published. The two constructed ethnographic profiles (named after places: Mulatos, Caribia) keep their titles.

## Graph construction and grouping rules

Edges drawn from source data (all undirected, never causal):

- `actor_initiative` / `initiative_initiative` — shared interconnection id (association, not strength or cooperation).
- `programme_membership`, `ecosystem_registration`, `initiative_programme` — **shared affiliation** (dashed). Hidden by default in the relational view.
- `responsible_entity` — source field on the initiative.

Edges added by curation: `episode_initiative` (reported episode), `episode_actor` (initiative-level association; attendance not asserted), `episode_function` (functional interpretation). System-map arrows are the only directed, signed links and are drawn only in System dynamics.

Grouping rules (named in the UI each time):

- **Initiative cluster** = connected components of source initiative–initiative interconnections → Agro-enterprise & finance (253, 254, 255, 259, 267), Tourism (250, 251, 260, 268), and two unlinked initiatives (252, 258).
- **Relational / shared initiatives** — organizations sit with the component(s) of the initiatives they are associated with; linked to both → bridge group; no initiative link → "registered or listed" group (collapsed on first load).
- **Relational / programme network** — by registering/listing programme network; several → own group.
- **Relational / episode context** — one episode's initiatives and initiative-level organizations at the centre.
- **Functional repertoire** — *All functions*: each episode sits between the hubs of the functions its readings name. *One function*: episodes with that reading gather in the analyst configurations of `functional-configurations.json`; others recede; initiatives/organizations are pulled to the configuration(s) of their episodes. Node identity and positions persist across functions and tabs (shared position store); transitions update force targets, never reseed.
- **Portfolio** — by initiative cluster, programme network, or function (link labels = number of supporting episodes).

Knowledge recombination vs coordination: almost every curated episode carries a coordination reading, so the difference is in the configurations (e.g. the milk episode groups with technical know-how under knowledge, but with buyer articulation under coordination). Mutual support rests on a single episode (`ep-fund`); the thin base is shown as such.

## Round 2 additions

**Territory story** (`content/territory.json` → `story`, `programme`): four short steps (place, challenge, Work4Progress response, question of the demo), each with verified report quotes. The attribution of Work4Progress to Fundación "la Caixa" rests on the data source itself: the snapshot was retrieved from the public API of `work4progress.fundacionlacaixa.org` (see `manifest.json` in the raw archive). The two reports do not name the foundation.

**Timeline**: lanes for programme-network durations, initiative start → end, listening/perception records per month (two phases: Mar–Jun 2024 and Mar–Jul 2026) and organization records. Organization dates are the dates of their records (31 of 48 display nodes have one; 17 have none and can be included with a toggle). Relations have no date in the source (`createdAt` is data-entry time), so the time-sliced network shows a link once both ends are recorded — it does not show when a relation began.

**Listening explainer**: five steps (field listening → reports → excerpt selection → verification → use). Counts come from the data; `src/data/generated/verification.json` is written by `npm run check:data`. The reports and the platform records are separate products; no report excerpt is linked to a specific listening record.

**Relational network as a hypergraph** (`src/data/hypergraph.ts`): hyperedges are initiatives (organizations with `actor_initiative`/`responsible_entity` associations), programme networks (`programme_membership`/`ecosystem_registration`) and, optionally, episodes (initiative-level actor lists). Contexts start collapsed; shared-organization links between contexts are drawn when they share ≥ N organizations (default 2). Centrality menu:
- *Hyperdegree*: number of included contexts per organization (contexts: size).
- *Betweenness*: Brandes, unweighted, on the bipartite organization–context incidence graph, normalised by (n−1)(n−2).
- *Eigenvector*: power iteration on the organization projection weighted by number of shared contexts (max = 1); for contexts, the mean of member scores.

These measures describe position in administrative records of association, not influence, trust or observed collaboration. Results depend on which context types are included. They are not used in any functional reading.

**Functional repertoire levels**: L0 four functions (link label = number of episodes whose readings name both functions); L1 configurations of a function (analyst grouping); L2 episodes; L3 initiatives and initiative-level organizations. Same node ids across levels and tabs.

## Decisions (Pedro Portela, 9 Nov 2026)

1. Episodes of the ALC listening process enter the repertoire (they are not set apart as "the instrument").
2. Institutional responses (water consortium, access quotas) count as configurations.
3. A shared resource imposed by an institution (water) is **not** read as mutual support → coded coordination only.
4. The three municipal contrast sessions are three episodes, shown as one series.
5. Level-C practices (only a project self-description) are left out of the app.
6. Sources 0088 and 0145 are one source (9 identical excerpts); the original code is kept on each excerpt.

## Display rules specific to Urdaibai

- **Large territory:** Portfolio and Relational open on the five featured entries (two are curated context: a community practice and the listening process). "+ one step out" adds initiatives sharing a listed organisation or a source interconnection; "All 210 records" shows everything; the hypergraph also takes single additions by name.
- **Initiative clusters:** connected components of source initiative–initiative interconnections; all initiatives without such a link form one group.
- **Context group** in the Portfolio is the K-tool council label (79 records are attributed to the whole comarca).
- **Lead / associated agent** listings are administrative metadata, not episode participation. The one pair listed in both fields is drawn once with both labels.
- **Timeline:** K-tool dates are record dates (mostly 2025). The lanes show initiative records per month, dated episodes, two listening-process milestones and organisation record dates. The time-sliced network shows dated episodes with their participants (an incidence view), not every record.
- **Listening:** 1,229 selected excerpts, Basque or Spanish as recorded. The locale field does not establish language and is not used as a filter. Search covers text, theme, source code and record ID. Five narrative-profile references and ten supplied-interview references are separate record types.
- **System dynamics:** the supplied exploratory map. Default view shows the 8 interview/synthesis links; 8 modeller hypotheses are optional; a loop is labelled shown only when all its links are visible. Interview references are reference-only.
- **Money:** only the Ortube €190,000 provincial contribution (reported, towards part of the work; not total cost, not verified disbursement). The one K-tool budget field (Gure Artea awards, 60,000) is shown as source metadata only.
